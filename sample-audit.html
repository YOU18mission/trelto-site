AI Visibility Audit (sample) — a local HVAC company

November 21, 2026. A method sample, not a client report.

This is independent research, not a paid engagement. We ran this audit from public sources on November 21, 2026 to show what a Trelto report looks like. The company is not a client, and we have not contacted them. Findings are from a single run on one engine (Exa). Other engines were not tested and are marked unverified.

A further caveat from the research: public permit history suggests this company may be mainly commercial or mechanical. That should be confirmed before it would ever be treated as a residential HVAC prospect. It also has no public email or website.

15/100 Invisible

Headline: 15/100 (Invisible) on the one engine we could test (Exa). This local HVAC company was named in 1 of 7 local buying questions (7th of 9 names, in the furnace-quotes answer). It has no website, and AI says it has “no customer reviews or ratings.” Meanwhile B2C Air Services and Wright Heating were each named 3 times.

Fix-first list

  1. Build a simple website (five pages: Home, Heating, Cooling, Areas Served, Contact) with HVACBusiness JSON-LD (address, phones, license number, areaServed: the towns it actually serves). This is the single biggest gap: AI has nothing first-party to cite.
  2. Claim and complete Google Business Profile: add heating categories (furnace repair and installation), not just AC. Unverified: we did not view the GBP listing.
  3. Get the first 15–25 reviews (Google first, then BBB and Angi) from past customers. AI currently says “no reviews.”
  4. Fix directory descriptions that say AC-only (hvacservice.io, BuildZoom, ohiobiz) so heating work shows up.
  5. Publish a town page and FAQ answering “furnace replacement cost in [town],” “[town] HVAC permit,” and “heat pump vs furnace in our region” (Exa already surfaces the local permit question from a competitor page).

Quick wins (this week)

  • Claim GBP and add “Furnace repair service” and “Heating contractor” categories.
  • Text 10 recent customers a review link.
  • Ask BuildZoom and hvacservice.io to update services to include heating.
  • Put the license number and “family-owned in [town] since ____” anywhere they have a profile.

30-60-90 plan

  • Days 1–30: website live with schema and town and area pages; GBP complete; 10+ reviews; directory fixes on the top 5 cited sites (BuildZoom, hvacservice.io, BBB, Angi, Yelp).
  • Days 31–60: FAQ and cost content; 25+ reviews; neighbor-town pages; re-run the 7-prompt set, 3 runs per engine. Target: named in at least 3 of 7 on Exa.
  • Days 61–90: monthly re-run; add any engine that becomes queryable (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini); go after the prompts where competitors still win (emergency, heat pumps).

Method and limits

Single Exa run on November 21, 2026. AI answers vary run to run (BrightLocal, September 2026: 20–33% overlap on repeats), so treat 1-of-7 as directional. No location spoofing. All other engines unverified. Google Business Profile and Google Maps rank were not checked.

A paid Fix Sprint baseline uses 10 agreed prompts, the engines we can query, and 3 runs each — not a single run like this sample.
