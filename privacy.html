Privacy policy

Last updated November 22, 2026. This is written in plain language on purpose. Trelto is a service of Geyer Digital Support LLC, based in Aurora, Ohio.

What this site collects

This website is a static site. It does not set marketing cookies, does not embed a tracking pixel, and does not use analytics on the pages themselves. Your browser will of course send standard technical data (IP address, browser, the page you asked for) to whatever host serves the files. That host’s logs are used to keep the site running, not to build a marketing profile.

What you send us

If you use the Snapshot form, the free AI visibility check form, or email hello@trelto.com, we receive the information you include: typically your name, email, phone if you give one, business name, website, city or towns, services, and any notes. We use that to run and send the Snapshot or audit, and to answer your questions. If you later buy an audit, a Fix Sprint, or Care, we also keep what we need to do the work and to handle billing and refunds (including records from our payment processor, Stripe, once that is set up).

Until a Google Form (or another named form provider) is wired on a page, the Snapshot form and the free AI visibility check form open your email app and send the request to hello@trelto.com. Once a Google Form is wired, Google is the named processor: your responses are stored in a Google Sheet we read in order to run and send the Snapshot. We will update this page if a different provider is added.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not buy or use third-party prospect lists for spam.
  • We do not publish your Snapshot, audit, or results without your permission. The sample audit on this site is independent public research, not a client report.
  • We do not need your bank login, card numbers (those go to Stripe, not to us), or ad-account passwords to do the core work.

How long we keep it

Emails, reports, and billing records are kept as long as we need them to do the work, honor a guarantee or refund, and meet ordinary record-keeping rules. If you want your inquiry deleted and we are not required to keep it, write to hello@trelto.com and we will do that.

Your rights

You can ask what we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing hello@trelto.com. If you are in a place with additional privacy rights, we will honor those requests as the law requires.

Children

This site is for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update this page and the date above.
