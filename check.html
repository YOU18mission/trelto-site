Free AI visibility check

This is a free check for local HVAC shops. Tell us the shop and the city. We’ll email a short Snapshot within 48 hours of a complete request.

We run the same logged-out baseline method we already use: six local Google Maps searches, plus the AI tools we can actually query without a login. Today that is ChatGPT and Google AI Mode. We will not claim we tested Perplexity or Copilot unless a login exists, and anything we could not test will be marked unverified.

The Snapshot says whether the shop was named, who was named instead, and a few quick wins. It is not the $97 audit, and it has no results guarantee.

Or write directly to hello@trelto.com. We use your details only to run and send the check. See the privacy policy and terms.

What you’ll get

A short Snapshot, by email, within 48 hours of a complete request. It will list the Google Maps searches and the AI tools we actually ran, whether you were named, who was named instead, and a few quick wins.

Anything we could not test will be marked unverified. This is not the $97 audit, and it has no results guarantee. We won’t follow up with a sales call. If you want the full audit after you read the Snapshot, you can reply to the email.
