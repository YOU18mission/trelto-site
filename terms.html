1. The services

Trelto offers answer engine optimization work for local businesses, starting with residential HVAC companies. The current offer is:

  • AI Snapshot — a one-page, single-run check of buyer questions on the engines we can query, plus three quick wins. Free for founding HVAC shops, at our discretion, and not a guarantee of results.
  • Founding Sprint — for up to three founding clients: the 60-day Local SEO + AI visibility sprint for a $297 founding fee paid at signup (covers the full 60 days), refunded in full if the day-60 result is not a PASS. See section 5.
  • Care (optional) — $99 per month, starting after the 60-day sprint (day 61), month-to-month, cancel anytime by email before the next renewal. Care includes a monthly re-measure of the same Maps keywords and AI prompts with a 1-page scorecard, Google Business Profile upkeep, review monitoring and owner nudges, and fixes when something breaks.
  • Growth (optional) — $397 per month, starting after the 60-day sprint (day 61), month-to-month, cancel anytime by email before the next renewal. Growth includes everything in Care, plus one new or refreshed town or service page a month and up to 6 more tracked keywords.
  • One-time add-ons — extra town or service page, $149 each; review-request setup, $149; citation cleanup, $199.
  • After the sprint — the sprint is one-time. Nothing is billed after day 60 unless you choose Care, Growth, or an add-on. You can choose on day 60, when you receive the day-60 results.

We may decline work that is outside this niche, that we cannot measure honestly, or that would exceed the number of sprints we can deliver carefully. A paid engagement starts when we email confirmation after payment, not when you submit the Snapshot form.

2. What we can and cannot promise

We only score and baseline engines we can actually query at the time. Unverified engines are marked as such and are not scored. AI answers change from run to run. We do not promise a particular rank in ChatGPT, Google, or any other product, and we do not promise a number of leads or jobs. The only performance promise is the founding sprint 60-day guarantee below.

3. Monthly plans and add-ons

Care and Growth are optional and start only if you choose them, beginning on day 61 after the sprint. They are month-to-month: cancel anytime by email before the next renewal and you will not be billed again. Add-ons are one-time. Care, Growth, and add-on fees are separate and are not covered by the 60-day guarantee.

4. Founding sprint 60-day guarantee

Before the sprint starts, we will agree in writing (email is enough) on 6 Google Maps and Google searches, 10 buyer questions, and the AI tools we will query (ChatGPT, Google AI, Perplexity, and Copilot, unless we agree otherwise). We will measure those on day 0, day 30, and day 60, using the same keywords, prompts, engines, and method each time, and we will show you the raw results. Day 30 is a progress check only. The guarantee test is the day-60 measurement compared with day 0.

The result is a PASS if any one of the following is true when day 60 is compared with day 0:

  • The number of AI named cells has increased by 2 or more. A named cell is a (question × tool) combination in which your business is named in at least 2 of the 3 runs (maximum 32 cells).
  • The total number of AI mentions (runs in which your business is named) has increased by 4 or more (maximum 96 mentions).
  • Your average Google Maps position across the 6 agreed keywords has improved by 2 or more positions. A result outside the first 20 is counted as position 21.
  • The number of those keywords for which your business appears in the Google Maps top 3 has increased by 1 or more.

If the result is not a PASS, we will refund the $297 founding fee in full. “Named” means your business is identified in the answer, not merely that a directory mentioning you is cited.

The guarantee does not apply if we cannot complete the agreed work because access, information, or approvals we requested in the intake were not provided in a reasonable time, or if the business name, location, or site changes in a way that breaks the comparison. During the founding period, a FAIL still means a founding client gets the $297 founding fee back in full and keeps the work (see section 5). Care, Growth, and add-on fees are separate and are not covered by this guarantee.
