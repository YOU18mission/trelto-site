1. The services

Trelto offers answer engine optimization work for local businesses, starting with residential HVAC companies in Northeast Ohio. The current offer is:

  • AI Snapshot — a one-page, single-run check of buyer questions on the engines we can query, plus three quick wins. Free for founding HVAC shops in Northeast Ohio, at our discretion, and not a guarantee of results.
  • Full AI Visibility Audit — $97. A scored report and a short walkthrough video, as described on the site.
  • Founding Sprint — for up to three founding clients: the 60-day Local SEO + AI visibility sprint with no upfront fee, and $375 due only if the day-60 result is a PASS. See section 5.

We may decline work that is outside this niche, that we cannot measure honestly, or that would exceed the number of sprints we can deliver carefully. A paid engagement starts when we email confirmation after payment, not when you submit the Snapshot form.

2. What we can and cannot promise

We only score and baseline engines we can actually query at the time. Unverified engines are marked as such and are not scored. AI answers change from run to run. We do not promise a particular rank in ChatGPT, Google, or any other product, and we do not promise a number of leads or jobs. The only performance promise is the Fix Sprint guarantee below.

3. Audit refund

If you pay $97 for the Full AI Visibility Audit and you tell us it was not useful, we will refund that $97 in full. No argument. Ask by email to hello@trelto.com after you have received the report. 4. Fix Sprint 60-day guarantee

Before the sprint starts, we will agree in writing (email is enough) on 6 Google Maps and Google searches, 10 buyer questions, and the AI tools we will query (ChatGPT, Google AI, Perplexity, and Copilot, unless we agree otherwise). We will measure those on day 0, day 30, and day 60, using the same keywords, prompts, engines, and method each time, and we will show you the raw results. Day 30 is a progress check only. The guarantee test is the day-60 measurement compared with day 0.

The result is a PASS if any one of the following is true when day 60 is compared with day 0:

  • The number of AI named cells has increased by 2 or more. A named cell is a (question × tool) combination in which your business is named in at least 2 of the 3 runs (maximum 32 cells).
  • The total number of AI mentions (runs in which your business is named) has increased by 4 or more (maximum 96 mentions).
  • Your average Google Maps position across the 6 agreed keywords has improved by 2 or more positions. A result outside the first 20 is counted as position 21.
  • The number of those keywords for which your business appears in the Google Maps top 3 has increased by 1 or more.

If the result is not a PASS, we will refund the Fix Sprint fee in full. “Named” means your business is identified in the answer, not merely that a directory mentioning you is cited.

The guarantee does not apply if we cannot complete the agreed work because access, information, or approvals we requested in the intake were not provided in a reasonable time, or if the business name, location, or site changes in a way that breaks the comparison. During the founding period, a FAIL still means a founding client owes nothing and keeps the work (see section 5). Care plan fees are separate and are not covered by this guarantee.
