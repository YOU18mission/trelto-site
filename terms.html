Last updated November 21, 2026. These terms are between you and Geyer Digital Support LLC, doing business as Trelto, for work we agree to do by email. Ohio law governs, and ordinary disputes are handled in Ohio courts, unless the law requires otherwise.

1. The services

Trelto offers answer engine optimization work for local businesses, starting with residential HVAC companies in Northeast Ohio. The current offer is:

  • AI Snapshot — a one-page, single-run check of buyer questions on the engines we can query, plus three quick wins. Free for founding HVAC shops in Northeast Ohio, at our discretion, and not a guarantee of results.
  • Full AI Visibility Audit — $97. A scored report and a short walkthrough video, as described on the site.
  • Fix Sprint — $750 intro price for founding clients, for about 30 days of the work described on the site. The price is scheduled to rise to $1,250 after three published case studies and $2,000 after six. If you buy a sprint within 14 days of paying for the audit, the $97 is credited toward the sprint.
  • Care plan — $297 per month, month-to-month, cancel anytime by email before the next renewal.

We may decline work that is outside this niche, that we cannot measure honestly, or that would exceed the number of sprints we can deliver carefully. A paid engagement starts when we email confirmation after payment, not when you submit the Snapshot form.

2. What we can and cannot promise

We only score and baseline engines we can actually query at the time. Unverified engines are marked as such and are not scored. AI answers change from run to run. We do not promise a particular rank in ChatGPT, Google, or any other product, and we do not promise a number of leads or jobs. The only performance promise is the Fix Sprint guarantee below.

3. Audit refund

If you pay $97 for the Full AI Visibility Audit and you tell us it was not useful, we will refund that $97 in full. No argument. Ask by email to hello@trelto.com after you have received the report. If the $97 was already credited toward a Fix Sprint, it is no longer separately refundable as an audit.

4. Fix Sprint 60-day guarantee

Before the sprint starts, we will agree in writing (email is enough) on a set of 10 buyer prompts and on the engines we can query. We will run each prompt three times and save that as the day-0 baseline: the number of those prompts on which your business is named at least once across the three runs of that prompt, rolled up as described in the baseline email.

At day 60 we run the same prompts, on the same engines, three times each. If you are named on fewer prompts, or on the same number of prompts, as at day 0, we will refund the Fix Sprint fee in full. “Named” means your business is identified in the answer, not merely that a directory mentioning you is cited.

The guarantee does not apply if we cannot complete the agreed work because access, information, or approvals we requested in the intake were not provided in a reasonable time, or if the business name, location, or site changes in a way that breaks the comparison. Care plan fees are separate and are not covered by this guarantee.
