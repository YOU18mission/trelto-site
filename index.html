The problem

Homeowners are asking AI who to call. The list is short.

This is not a theory. In March 2026, BrightLocal surveyed 1,002 U.S. adults and found that 45% had used an AI tool for a local-business recommendation in the past year, up from 6% the year before. ChatGPT was the tool 31% of them used; Google AI Mode was 23%. A later BrightLocal study of 200,000 local AI prompts found that answers typically name only two to four businesses, and that business websites make up 42% of the citations.

FAQ

Straight answers

What is answer engine optimization (AEO)?

It’s the work of making a business easy for AI answer tools — ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and others — to name and describe accurately when someone asks a buying question. It overlaps with local SEO (your site, your Google Business Profile, your reviews, your directories), but the test is different: when a homeowner asks who to call, are you in the answer?

Do you only work with HVAC companies?

Right now, yes. Residential HVAC in Northeast Ohio is the first niche, on purpose. If the work holds up, we can take on nearby trades later. If you’re outside HVAC or outside this area, you can still write, but we may decline until we can do the job properly.

Which AI engines do you actually test?

Only engines we can query for that report. Every Snapshot and audit includes an engines table. Anything we could not test is marked unverified and is not scored. We will not estimate what ChatGPT or Google “probably” said. As more engines become testable, they get added to new reports and to sprint baselines.

How is the free Snapshot different from the $97 audit?

The Snapshot is one page: seven buyer questions, who was named, and three quick wins, from a single run. The $97 audit is the full report — score, heat map, competitors, citations, a ranked fix list, a 30-60-90 plan — plus a short walkthrough video. If you buy a Fix Sprint within 14 days of the audit, the $97 is credited toward it.

What does the 60-day guarantee actually mean?

It’s a count, not a vibe. We agree on 10 prompts and the engines we can query, run each prompt three times at day 0, and do the same at day 60. If you are not named on more of those prompts than at the start, the Fix Sprint is refunded in full. AI answers do vary from run to run, which is why we use three runs and a fixed prompt set.

Do I have to hand over my website?

No. After a sprint is paid, we send an intake form. You can give site access, or we can write the pages and schema and send them to your web person. We also ask for a Google Business Profile manager invite when that’s possible. We never need bank access or the ability to spend your ad budget.

Is this the same as running ads?

No. The Snapshot, audit, sprint, and Care plan are about organic AI answers and the public information those tools cite. We may offer a Google Ads add-on later, only after we have Care clients who want it, and ad spend would be paid by you in your own account. It is not part of the $750 sprint.
