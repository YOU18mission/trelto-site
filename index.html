What we find

Real problems from checks we ran this month.

These come from checks we ran on local service company websites in November 2026. Company names are withheld. Each one is the kind of issue that quietly keeps a good business out of search and AI answers.

Business data conflict

Two different addresses, one business

The structured data on the site listed a different business name and address than the contact page, and its website link was broken. Search engines and AI tools get mixed signals about where the company is.

Missing location signals

An established company that never names its town

The homepage never mentioned the city it serves in its visible text, and the site had no city or service-area pages at all.

Missing structure

No business markup, no FAQ

An established company’s site had no LocalBusiness markup and no FAQ page, two of the easiest ways to tell Google and AI tools what you do and where.

Every check lists the date, the searches, and the tools we ran. Anything we couldn’t measure is marked unverified, never guessed.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What exactly do you do?

We do search engine optimization (SEO) and AI search optimization for local service companies. That means fixing the things that decide whether you show up and get picked when a customer searches: your Google Business Profile, your website pages and structured data, your reviews process, and the directory listings that Google and AI tools read. The goal is more calls, form leads, and booked jobs.

What is AI search optimization (AEO)?

Customers now ask Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini, ChatGPT, and Perplexity who to hire. Those tools usually name only a few companies. AI search optimization, also called answer engine optimization (AEO), is the work of making your business easy for those tools to find, trust, and recommend. It builds on the same foundations as local SEO.

Do you work on Gemini and Google AI Overviews too?

Yes. Google Search, Google Maps, AI Overviews, AI Mode, and Gemini all draw on your Google Business Profile, your website, and what the rest of the web says about you. We work on all of them, plus ChatGPT and Perplexity.

Who is this for?

Established local service companies with a team and a marketing budget that want more inbound leads: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, home services, med spas, clinics, gyms, and similar businesses.

Can you guarantee a #1 ranking?

No, and you should be careful with anyone who does. Google and the AI tools are run by other companies and their answers change. What we do promise is in writing: we measure agreed searches and questions on day 0 and day 60, and if the founding sprint does not pass, you get the $297 back and keep the work. The exact rule is in our terms.

How do you prove it is working?

Before we start, we agree on 6 Google Maps and Google searches and 10 buyer questions. We run each AI question 3 times, on the same tools, on day 0, day 30, and day 60, and send you the dated raw results. You see the before and after yourself.

How long until we see results?

Some fixes, like profile and website corrections, can show up within weeks. Rankings and AI answers usually take longer, and nothing is promised in the first 2 to 3 weeks. That is why the sprint runs 60 days and is measured at day 30 and day 60.

Do I need a new website?

Usually not. We can make changes on your existing site with editor access, or send ready-to-paste pages and code to your web person. We never ask for passwords.

Do we have to get on a call?

No. Most clients prefer email, so your team is not pulled off jobs. Everything, including reports, is in writing.
